ADAPTATION (everything that differs from the source text)

- Page geometry: DL4NLP is 4:3, this course is 16:9 (about 17pt less height), so
  34 frames overflowed. Fixed per frame by scaling \includegraphics sizes by 0.85
  (twice where needed; 26 of the edits are the 13-page ARLM toy-example build-up)
  and, for two text-only frames ("Approximate Parameter Count (2)", "(3) bertology
  summary"), a \small / \footnotesize line marked "% fit16:9". No text was changed.
- Source bugs fixed: figure/52-albert-sota.png and ../chapter01-basics/figure/
  seq2seq.png are .PNG on disk (only resolves on case-insensitive filesystems);
  copied under the referenced names, cross-chapter path flattened to figure/.
- Preamble: DL4NLP macros (\citebutton, \ques, \myblue, \blu, \org, \devour,
  \warning) and \usepackage{animate} added to the deck. The course's typewriter
  font (laett) has no bold shape, which DL4NLP's \textbf{\texttt{...}} needs; a
  deck-local font-shape substitution maps it to regular.
