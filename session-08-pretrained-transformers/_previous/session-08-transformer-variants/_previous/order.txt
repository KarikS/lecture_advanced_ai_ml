This session compiles to a single deck: slides-transformer-variants.tex

Built 2026-09-27, as the second half of splitting the original single "Transformer"
session into two (session 7: Attention & Transformer Architecture; this one: BERT/T5
variants), per the scope conversation in git history / the conversation this was built
in. Source material is DL4NLP chapters 4-6 (~206 raw frames across 11 files), which is
far more than a single session can carry -- this deck condenses aggressively rather than
reproducing it close to verbatim (unlike session 7's first pass, which overshot its page
target by including nearly everything). Concretely: many multi-frame figure walkthroughs,
ablation-by-ablation result frames, and near-duplicate animated-buildup frames were
collapsed into single frames stating the finding, with the source cited so the full
detail is still one click away.

Sections, in order, with sourcing:

1. Recap: The Three Transformer Families [adapted from original text first written for
   session 7] -- opens with the same taxonomy table before diving into detail, so the
   encoder-only/decoder-only/encoder-decoder distinction the user asked to make
   structurally prominent carries across both sessions, not just session 7.

2. Transformers Beyond NLP [RELOCATED from session 7's original "Transformer Motivation
   & Applications" section, not newly authored] -- Vision Transformer, CLIP x2, Flamingo,
   GPT-4. Moved here (with a new one-frame bridge) specifically to address a scope
   concern raised mid-conversation: sessions 7-9 read as "general Transformer course,
   NLP only" once 8-9 are BERT/T5 and GPT. Opening this session with non-NLP evidence
   before its otherwise-100%-NLP content (BERT, RoBERTa, ALBERT, DistilBERT, ELECTRA, T5)
   was judged a better fix than leaving the frames in session 7, where they didn't serve
   this purpose. See session 7's order.txt for the corresponding removal.

3. BERT: Predecessors and Core Facts [dl4nlp/chapter04-bert/{slides-43-corefacts,
   slides-44-pretrain-finetune}.tex] -- the 5-frame animated "Predecessors of BERT"
   build-up (chapter05's identical device, "successors" chains, repeats 3-5 times across
   chapters 4-6) condensed to 1 frame using only the final, most complete build-up image;
   ELMo/ULMFiT/GPT shortcomings kept as prose. BERT key facts, the ELMo/GPT/BERT
   comparison, architecture numbers, causality remark, input/output embeddings, and the
   parameter-count derivation (explicit callback to session 7's own derivation) kept
   close to complete -- this is the flagship encoder-only model, the one place in this
   session where depth matters most.

4. Autoregressive vs. Masked Language Modeling [dl4nlp/chapter04-bert/
   slides-41-arlm-mlm.tex] -- the single biggest cut in this session: the original
   ARLM "toy example" is 13 near-identical frames, each showing one more page of the
   same animated build-up PDF (meant for live reveal during a lecture, not 13 static
   slides in a deck people read). Condensed to 1 frame using the final page (the
   complete, fully-built-up sentence), which shows the same information. Two other
   pairs of near-identical "Examples of ARLMs" frames similarly merged.

5. Pre-Training and Fine-Tuning BERT [dl4nlp/chapter04-bert/slides-44-pretrain-finetune,
   slides-42-metrics.tex] -- MLM/NSP mechanics and BERT's actual pretrain/fine-tune
   recipe kept, condensed from ~14 frames to 5 (the three near-identical MLM masking
   examples merged into one worked example; the two fine-tuning figures merged). From
   slides-42-metrics.tex (20 raw frames), only perplexity was kept (2 frames, condensed
   from 5) -- the ranking-metrics content (MAP/MRR) and the generated-text-evaluation
   survey (BLEU/ROUGE/BERTScore/human eval) are generic NLP-evaluation material, only
   loosely BERT-specific, and were judged not worth the page budget here.

6. Self-Supervision and Transfer Learning [dl4nlp/chapter04-bert/
   slides-45-transferlearning-selfsup.tex] -- condensed from 11 frames to 3: kept the
   feature-based-vs-fine-tuning distinction and the self-supervision definition (both
   genuinely load-bearing concepts used throughout this session), cut the
   transductive/inductive transfer-learning taxonomy (3 frames, generic ML-transfer-
   learning content, not specific to this session's models) and the multilinguality
   remark (already covered, better, as BERTology's language-diversity limitation next).

7. BERTology: What Does BERT Actually Learn? [dl4nlp/chapter05-bert-based/
   slides-51-bertology.tex] -- condensed from 19 frames to 7. Kept: the implications
   framing, language diversity, max sequence length (a direct callback to session 7's
   O(n^2) discussion), bias/StereoSet, and a summary of the two headline BERTology
   findings (Clark et al.'s attention-pattern analysis, Michel et al.'s head-pruning
   result) plus the Jain & Wallace caveat. Cut: the individual attention-pattern figures
   (3 frames) and head-pruning figures (3 frames) in favor of stating the findings
   directly with a citation, and the "pretrain-finetune + transformer" paradigm-shift
   frame (redundant with points made elsewhere in this session).

8. RoBERTa [dl4nlp/chapter05-bert-based/slides-52-bert-based.tex, first half] --
   condensed from ~8 frames to 2: dynamic masking, dropping NSP, and more/longer
   training as the three changes, with one figure. The repeated "Successors of BERT"
   animated timeline (4 frames, same device as BERT's own predecessor timeline) was cut
   entirely as duplicate framing.

9. ALBERT [same file, second half] -- condensed from ~8 frames to 2: the E/H
   disentanglement argument and cross-layer parameter sharing kept (genuine architectural
   ideas), sentence-order prediction kept as a one-line contrast with NSP, the detailed
   ablation-by-ablation walkthrough (3 frames) and n-gram-masking detail (1 frame) cut in
   favor of stating the results.

10. DistilBERT [dl4nlp/chapter05-bert-based/slides-53-distilbert.tex] -- condensed from
    16 frames to 2: kept the model-compression motivation, soft/hard targets with
    temperature, DistilBERT's specific recipe, and headline results/ablations. Cut the
    "related approaches" frames on quantization and pruning (2 frames) -- interesting but
    not DistilBERT-specific, and this session already cut similarly-tangential material
    elsewhere for the same reason.

11. ELECTRA [dl4nlp/chapter06-post-bert-t5/slides-61-postbert.tex, first half] --
    condensed from 7 frames to 2: the generator/discriminator setup, why a smaller
    generator works better, and the headline compute-efficiency result. XLNet/permutation
    language modeling (same file, second half, 13 frames on content-stream/query-stream
    attention mechanics) was cut to a single one-sentence mention under "Self-Supervised
    Objectives, Beyond MLM" -- a deliberate scope call: XLNet's mechanics are a genuine
    rabbit hole and it is less central to this session's BERT/T5 spine than RoBERTa,
    ALBERT, DistilBERT, or ELECTRA.

12. From BERT to Text-to-Text / T5 [dl4nlp/chapter06-post-bert-t5/{slides-62-text2text,
    slides-63-t5}.tex] -- condensed from ~28 frames to 10. The "Problems with BERT"
    motivation (8 frames of Socratic build-up) condensed to 2 frames stating the two
    core problems directly. The text-to-text idea and worked examples condensed from 9
    frames to 1. T5 itself kept fairly complete (architecture, pretraining objective, C4,
    experiments/conclusions, parameter count, following the same session-7-callback style
    as BERT's parameter count) since T5 is this session's flagship encoder-decoder model
    -- but the three separate GLUE/SuperGLUE/MT benchmark-figure frames were condensed
    into one sentence in the conclusions frame, and the non-standard-head-dimension 3B/11B
    parameter-count derivation (2 frames of algebra) was cut as a scaling detail beyond
    this session's scope, noted as a footnote instead.

13. Encoder-Decoder Beyond NLP: Whisper [NEW, freshly drafted, 1 frame] -- Whisper
    (Radford et al., 2022) as the encoder-decoder analog to ViT's encoder-only example,
    reinforcing the "this isn't NLP-specific" framing a second time, right where the
    encoder-decoder deep dive (T5) ends. No source material existed for this in either
    i2dl or DL4NLP; drafted fresh and cited directly to the original paper.

14. Closing Synthesis [NEW, original text, 1 frame] -- the three-families table one final
    time, now filled in with every model this session covered plus a GPT/decoder-only
    row (even though GPT itself is session 9's subject), so the picture is complete.

Not included at all: slides-23-elmo.tex (ELMo itself; judged as belonging conceptually to
this session's BERT lineage rather than session 7's attention lineage, but its content
--- contextual embeddings via bidirectional LSTMs --- turned out to add little beyond
what "Predecessors of BERT" already covers in one paragraph, so it was left out
entirely rather than given its own frames).

Figures: 28 images copied from dl4nlp/slides/chapter0{4,5,6}-*/figure/ (flattened) plus
5 moved from session-07-transformer/figure/ (the relocated CV/multimodal frames). No
collisions found. Two figures (bert-problem.png, stereoset.png) are actually stored in
chapter05's figure/ directory despite being referenced from a chapter06 file
(slides-62-text2text.tex) --- the same kind of source/figure-directory mismatch session
6's order.txt flagged as a recurring gotcha in this material; caught by checking the
actual file location rather than assuming it matched the referencing chapter.

Macros: this deck needed \citebutton and \warning in addition to the \ques/tikzstyle set
already added in session 7 (dl4nlp's preamble_new.tex defines both; copied verbatim,
verified against the source file, not reconstructed from memory). Both were caught as
fatal "Undefined control sequence" errors on the first two build attempts and fixed
before a successful build, rather than discovered later.

Build: 105 pages, no errors, no missing figures, no undefined references (confirmed via
log grep for "not found"/"undefined"/"! LaTeX Error"/"Fatal error", and by rendering the
taxonomy table, the T5 parameter-count derivation, and the closing synthesis table with
pdftoppm --- all render cleanly with no overflow, unlike session 7's taxonomy table on
the first attempt there). This lands inside the ~80-110 page target on the first
successful build, in contrast to session 7's 156-165 pages -- the difference is
deliberate: this session was scoped for aggressive condensation from the start, given
the much larger raw-material surplus (206 frames) and the lesson already learned from
session 7's overshoot.
