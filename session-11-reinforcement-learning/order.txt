CORRECTIONS AND ADAPTATIONS IN THIS REVISION

- Remove inherited references to previous/next RL lectures.
- Agent/environment chronology: observe O_t,R_t before choosing A_t.
- Introduce Markov-chain transitions in the retained MRP definition;
  qualify stationary Markov policy and deterministic-optimum claims.
- Epsilon-greedy: test the queried action against a tie-broken greedy action.
- Q-learning: sample successor actions conditioned on S_{t+1}, not S_t.
- State finite tabular Q-learning convergence assumptions explicitly;
  do not extend the claim to DQN.
- DQN: terminal mask, fixed target parameters and periodic copying;
  split target and loss into two lines for readability.
- Action-value approximation: label the per-sample gradient as sampled,
  rather than equating it to the full expected gradient.
- Keep G_t for sampled returns. State the episodic policy gradient with
  explicit discounted trajectory sum; retain continuing objectives only as
  alternatives. REINFORCE scores are computed at the sampled policy before
  updating parameters, with the matching discount factor.
- QAC: initialise critic weights, fix action sampling notation and terminal
  handling; use assignment notation. Correct missing state argument in
  baseline derivation. Explain the state-value critic/TD-error advantage.
- Frame the application explicitly as InstructGPT (2022). Rewrite the RLHF
  objective explanation; distinguish reward-model parameters from policy
  parameters, frozen SFT reference, and the optional PPO-ptx mixture.
- The historical objective figure uses gamma as a pretraining mixture
  weight; explicitly distinguish it from the RL discount factor.
- Replace the long hallucination gallery with one factuality slide, and
  the broad alignment claims with a focused preference-representation slide.
- Reward hacking: KL may mitigate over-optimisation, not guarantee safety.
- GRPO spelling corrected; distinguish the optimiser from reward source,
  and connect verifiable rewards to session 8.
- Original 16:9 image fitting and Silver vector crops/TikZ redraws retained.
