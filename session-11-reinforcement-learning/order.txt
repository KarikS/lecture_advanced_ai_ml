slides/frames only (no summary frames replacing several source frames):

    5, 6, 7. https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/teaching/

    included in the license"). These sections are shared under CC BY-NC 4.0.
    The source PDFs are figure/silver-l{1,2,5,6,7}.pdf (downloaded 2026-11-07,
    unchanged); frames crop their figures from these files.

  Part B, sections 6-12 (RLHF)
    DL4NLP (SLDS, LMU Munich, CC BY 4.0), chapter10-rlhf:
      9itune.tex       sections "Original RLHF work: The backflipper",

PART A: HOW SILVER'S SLIDES ARE ADAPTED

- One frame per selected source slide, frame title = slide title.
  Slide numbers below are PDF page numbers of the source file.
- Text and formulas retypeset in the course font, in Silver's wording;
  Silver's red highlighting kept as \textcolor{red}; his "Definition" and
  "Theorem" boxes as beamer blocks.
- Figures (agent/environment diagrams, student Markov chain/MRP/MDP graphs,
  mazes, gridworlds, taxonomy diagrams, plots) are vector crops of the source
  page.
- The backup diagrams (L2 slides 20, 31, 32, 43, 44) are redrawn in TikZ: in
  the source PDF, two different subsets of CMMI10/CMSY10 share one font name,
  so pdfTeX drops the labels' glyphs when the page is cropped.
- Algorithms: the Q-learning box (L5 slide 38, an image in the source) and
  REINFORCE/QAC (L7 slides 21, 25) are typeset; same wording and layout.

ADAPTATION (everything that differs from the sources)

Part A
- Silver L1 slide 14 "Examples of Rewards": two columns in a widened box (the
  six examples do not fit one 16:9 column at the course's fixed sub-item size).
- Silver L6 slides 39-40: credit line "Figure: Mnih et al. (2015), Nature"
  added. Silver's slides show the DQN figures without a source; they are
  third-party content (see license note above).
- Typos/notation copied as in the source, e.g. L7 slide 25 "theta = theta +
  ..." (= instead of <-), L7 slide 41 "Each leads a stochastic gradient ascent
  algorithm", L5 slide 36 "max_a' gamma Q" order.
- Font size \small on L7 slides 12 (left column), 21 and 25 (algorithms).

Part B
- vbframe -> frame throughout (vbframe broke most image frames into a nearly
  empty first page plus the figure on a second page at 16:9).
- Figures fitted to 16:9, each marked "% fit16:9" in the .tex:
  height caps (keepaspectratio) on trainingprocess, threesteps, instructgpttable3b,
  webinterface1/2, rlhfdetails, questionsaboutcode, harmfulpromptfollowing,
  teenagesuicide, extractmemory1/2, gemini,3,flash,hallu; width 12/12.5cm ->
  \linewidth (11 figures); reward model frames: threesteps 4cm -> 3.2cm,
  rewardloss 10cm -> 8cm.
- \small on "Instructions to labelers ... (1)", "(2)" and "Can InstructGPT
  solve unseen tasks?".
- Reward hacking (2): "(see earlier evaluation chart: page 45)" ->
  "(see earlier evaluation chart: ``Improvement on four dimensions'')"
  (DL4NLP page number).
- figure/qwen37max,refusal.png is missing from the DL4NLP repository; it was
  extracted from DL4NLP's compiled 9rlhf.pdf (page 42), unchanged.
- Kept as in the source, worth a look when reviewing: "Reasoning" ends with
  "See lecture on reasoning" (session 08 covers reasoning); "Harmful prompts:
  Still a problem" shows a news photo on teenage suicide.
