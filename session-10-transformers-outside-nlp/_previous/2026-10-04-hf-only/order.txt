DEVIATIONS FROM THE SOURCE TEXT (all deliberate, all small)

- Citation buttons: author/year labels added to the paper links the course gives;
  all arXiv IDs and author names verified against arXiv metadata on 2026-10-04.
  One link is not in the course: Winoground (Thrush et al., 2022, arXiv 2204.03162),
  whose figure the course shows without a link.
- Distillation loss: the course shows it as an image (KL-Loss.png); typeset in LaTeX
  instead, with the symbols explained.
- TimeSformer: the course lists the first scheme as "Sparse Attention"; the paper
  (and the figure) call it "Space attention". Written as "Space".
- ViT cost: the course says "2,500 days on TPU-v3" and prices it per core-hour; the
  ViT paper's unit is TPUv3-core-days, so it is written "2,500 TPU-v3 days" at
  "$2 per TPU-v3 core-hour" (same arithmetic, $120,000).
- CLIP code: imports of PIL/requests and the image download omitted; the model,
  processor, prompts and softmax are the course's code.
- Omitted: links to Colab notebooks, Gradio demos, inference output dumps, and the
  vlm-intro page's poem.
