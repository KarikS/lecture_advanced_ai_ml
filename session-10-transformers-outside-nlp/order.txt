DEVIATIONS FROM THE SOURCE TEXT (all deliberate, all small)

Existing sections (unchanged since 2026-10-04):
- Citation buttons: author/year labels added to the paper links the HF course gives;
  arXiv IDs verified 2026-10-04. Winoground (Thrush et al., 2022, arXiv 2204.03162)
  is linked although the course shows its figure without a link.
- Distillation loss formula, TimeSformer "Space" attention, ViT cost units, CLIP code:
  see _previous/2026-10-04-hf-only/order.txt (distillation section now dropped).
New in 2026-10-18:
- LMU section 3: links only for keys the booklet cites and that resolve in its
  book.bib and on arXiv: Ramachandran et al. 2019 (1906.05909), Dosovitskiy et al.
  2020 (2010.11929), Xie et al. 2019 (1911.04252). Not linked: @TRANSFORMERS_NLP (not
  in book.bib) and @wang (book.bib resolves it to an unrelated DCASE2018
  acoustic-scenes paper -- a citation error in the booklet). The sentence "the
  pre-trained position embeddings can become sufficient" (garbled in the source) is
  not used; "ViT Hughe" written "ViT-Huge"; the year "2021" before "Google Brain
  team" omitted. Accuracy numbers and model sizes copied exactly.
- LMU sections 10 and 16: text-only, no citation buttons (model names only).
  Multipurpose comparison table: 5 of the source's 7 columns (unseen tasks and
  parameter counts omitted for width).
- Terms the course never introduces removed from LMU/HF frames: "mixture-of-experts
  layer" (MultiModel encoder, now described by its role: it receives the modality
  nets' input and feeds the I/O mixer and decoder), "perceiver architectures" (Flamingo), "Byte-Pair
  Encoding" (OFA), "SentencePiece" -> "text as tokens" (Gato), "(as in DALL-E)" (OFA),
  "non-maximum suppression" (MaskFormer; "spatial anchors" kept, explained on the
  DETR frame).

- [O] one original line on "ViT Method: The Encoder" (2026-10-18): "Newer ViTs update
  this block as discussed in session 9, e.g. SwiGLU and RoPE (EVA-02), QK-norm and
  parallel layers (ViT-22B)" -- so the LayerNorm / plain-MLP / absolute-position ViT
  does not read as current after session 9. Verified in the papers: EVA-02 (arXiv
  2303.11331) §2.1 uses SwiGLU, sub-LN and 2D RoPE; ViT-22B (arXiv 2302.05442) §2 uses
  parallel layers, QK normalization, no biases on QKV projections/LayerNorms.
