  Variant" (refers to CS336's own assignment).

  L3 3 "(what you implement)", L3 16 "(we will discuss this in far more detail
  later)", L3 36 "in 224n..", L3 42 "As a reminder, slide from 224n." The title-page
  figure is L3 5 cropped below its title.

  bullets + crops -- their figures would otherwise be too small to read, or (L3 4,

  Their text is therefore not editable in the .tex.

  crops or as small text lines; no links were added to source frames.
